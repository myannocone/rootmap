RootMap privacy

RootMap is a scoreboard for the fantasy leagues you connect. It shows who you are rooting for and against across those leagues.

Passwords

No passwords are saved or stored. Yahoo sign-in happens on Yahoo’s page. ESPN sign-in, in the iPhone app, happens on ESPN’s page. RootMap does not keep those passwords.

What stays on this device

Fantasy roster IDs, team names, and the starters needed to draw the board are stored locally on-device. Clearing data in the app removes them from that device.

What we do not sell

No personal telemetry or location tracking is sold. RootMap does not sell your leagues, your location, or a profile of you.
