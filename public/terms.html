RootMap Terms of Use

RootMap is a scoreboard for the fantasy leagues you connect. These terms cover use of the app, including the optional RootMap Pro subscription.

RootMap Pro

RootMap Pro is an auto-renewable subscription sold through the App Store. Plans offered in the app are a 6-month pass and a monthly plan. Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm the purchase.

The subscription renews unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your Apple ID is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel anytime in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

Free use of RootMap includes up to 2 active leagues. RootMap Pro removes that cap.
